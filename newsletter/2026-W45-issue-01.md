**Hi —**

Welcome to issue #1. Every week we verify prices, limits and terms across the Chinese model API ecosystem and publish what actually changed. Here's week one:

**1. Kimi is sunsetting K2 — plan your migration.**
K2 models stopped accepting new users on Oct 31; Moonshot plans full retirement by end of December 2026. If your stack still calls `kimi-k2.6` or `k2.7-code`, move to K3/K3.1 now — the K3 line gives you a 1M-token context and batch inference at 60% of standard price. Also new: `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed`, a 2–3× faster variant.
→ Provider page: /providers/kimi

**2. MiniMax's new flagship is $0.15 in / $0.60 out.**
MiniMax-M3.1-Flash launched with prompt-cache reads at $0.015/M. The older M3 now carries a "permanent 50% off" tag — the list price you see struck through is not the real one. A Priority tier (1.5× price) exists for latency-sensitive traffic.
→ Provider page: /providers/minimax

**3. The same Qwen model has different prices depending on which region serves you.**
`qwen3.8-max` costs $2.00/$6.00 (in/out per 1M) from Singapore, but $1.65/$4.951 from China (Beijing) and the HK/Frankfurt/Virginia/Tokyo regions. Some Global regions also run night discounts (22:00–08:00 UTC+8). Check which region your endpoint hits before comparing quotes.
→ Provider page: /providers/qwen

**4. Z.ai's Flash models are free, and caching is temporarily free too.**
GLM-4.7-Flash and GLM-4.5-Flash cost nothing right now; GLM-5.3 cached input is $0.26/M with storage free for a limited time. Good week to prototype on GLM.
→ Provider page: /providers/glm

**5. DeepSeek: no price changes — and a reminder.**
Off-peak (all US business hours) remains $0.15/$0.60 for deepseek-flash. If your reseller charges peak prices around the clock, you're overpaying.
→ Provider page: /providers/deepseek · New guide: /guides/openrouter-vs-direct
