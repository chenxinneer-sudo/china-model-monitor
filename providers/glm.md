# GLM (Z.ai / Zhipu) — Provider Page

**At a glance**
Zhipu's GLM family is the easiest Chinese frontier family to try from overseas: z.ai is an English-first platform with a **free Flash tier** and aggressive cached-input pricing. The consumer product (chat.z.ai) and the API platform share the docs site; a subscription "Coding Plan" exists for CLI coding tools ⚠️ details unverified.

### Pricing structure notes
- Flat per-token pricing — **no peak/off-peak windows** (unlike DeepSeek). Predictable.
- Cached-input discount is steep (~81% off GLM-5.3 input) → long system prompts / multi-turn agents are much cheaper than raw rates suggest.
